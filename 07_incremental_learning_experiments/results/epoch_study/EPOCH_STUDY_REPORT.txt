VERDICT (pseudo):
  - Old-class mAP rises MONOTONICALLY through E=24 -- no overfit turnover. The
    early-stop hypothesis (longer fine-tuning erodes old classes) is REFUTED for
    pseudo: the teacher's old-class pseudo-labels turn every epoch into joint
    old+new (rehearsal) training, so more epochs only converge the joint fit.
  - The only cost of more epochs is on NEW classes: new@.25 peaks at E=16 then
    slips at E=24, so overall@.25 peaks at E=16.
  - So: E=12 (paper default) stays sound; E=16 = best balanced; E=24 = best
    old-class retention. The swing across all of these is tenths of a point of
    mAP -> epoch budget is a weak lever; nothing below E=12 is ever justified.
